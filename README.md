Blue Sea Print: 6-Hour Survival Protocol

Immediate Action Plan: Hour 0 to First Meal

Hour 0-1: Read and Prepare

Document Scan Priority (30 minutes)
1. Safety Warnings (5 min): Read tidal warnings, dangerous species, legal minimums
2. First Meal Method (10 min): Focus on page showing "Tide Pool Survival Meal"
3. Tool Minimum (5 min): What can you build in 20 minutes
4. Safety Checklist (5 min): What NOT to do
5. Legal Quick Check (5 min): Avoid immediate trouble

Personal Assessment (30 minutes)
- [ ] What's in my pockets right now?
- [ ] What's within 50 meters? (Beach, rocks, tide pools)
- [ ] Check tide: Coming in or going out? (Tide table quick read)
- [ ] Sun position: How many daylight hours left?
- [ ] Current weather: Wind direction, water conditions

Hour 1-2: Immediate Tool Construction

Build These Three Tools (45 minutes)

Tool 1: Shell Knife (10 minutes)
1. Find flat stone with sharp edge
2. Find harder stone for hammer
3. Chip edge sharper
4. Test: Cut seaweed cleanly

Tool 2: Container (15 minutes)
- Option A: Large seashell (collect 2-3)
- Option B: Hat or shirt (remove if possible)
- Option C: Large leaf folded + secured with grass
- Test: Hold water without leaking

Tool 3: Digging Stick (5 minutes)
1. Find straight stick, wrist-thick, arm-length
2. Sharpen one end on rocks
3. Test: Dig in wet sand easily

Gather These Now (15 minutes)
- 5 meters of cordage (seaweed stems, grass, shoelaces)
- 10 fist-sized rocks for cooking/heating
- Dry tinder material (3 handfuls)
- Identification: Safe seaweed vs. dangerous types

Hour 2-4: First Food Harvest

Tide Pool Protocol (90 minutes)
Step 1: Find Right Pool (15 minutes)
- Look for pool that's:
  - At least knee-deep
  - Has visible life movement
  - Protected from breaking waves
  - Has rocky edges and seaweed

Step 2: Quick Harvest (45 minutes)
- Shellfish First (15 min):
  - Mussels/limpets on rocks (twist off)
  - Clams in sand (look for holes, dig fast)
  - Minimum target: 10-15 pieces

- Seaweed Second (15 min):
  - Green seaweed (sea lettuce)
  - Red seaweed (dulse)
  - Brown seaweed (kelp tips)
  - Minimum: 2 handfuls each type

- Trap Fish (15 min):
  - Build rock circle trap in pool
  - Chase small fish into trap
  - Use shirt/container as net
  - Target: 2-3 small fish (palm-sized)

Step 3: Emergency Backups (30 minutes)
If tide pool fails:
- Beach Worming: Look for worm casts at water's edge
- Crabs Under Rocks: Flip carefully, grab from behind
- Sea Urchins: In rocky crevices (spines dangerous)
- Periwinkles: On all rocky surfaces

Hour 4-5: Process and Cook

Immediate Processing (30 minutes)
Shellfish:
1. Rinse in seawater
2. Tap each: Discard any that stay open
3. For clams: Soak in seawater to purge sand

Seaweed:
1. Rinse in clean seawater
2. Sort by type
3. Chew test: If bitter, spit out

Fish:
1. Scale with shell knife
2. Gut (remove insides)
3. Remove gills
4. Keep liver/heart

Fire and Cooking (30 minutes)
No-Fire Method (if no lighter):
1. Sun Cooking: 
   - Place black rocks in sun 30+ minutes
   - Lay thin fish fillets on hot rocks
   - Cover with seaweed for steam

2. Ceviche Method:
   - Chop fish into small pieces
   - Soak in acidic juice (if citrus available)
   - Wait 30 minutes (eat while waiting for other food)

With Fire (if you have flame):
1. Build tiny fire (hand-sized)
2. Heat rocks in fire
3. Use as cooking surface
4. Cook shellfish until shells open

Eating Order (Critical)
1. First Bite: Small piece of seaweed (test body reaction)
2. Wait 15 minutes
3. Second Bite: Small piece of cooked shellfish
4. Wait 15 minutes
5. Main Meal: Mixed seaweed + protein

Hour 5-6: First Meal Achieved

Minimum Viable First Meal
- Protein: 5-10 shellfish OR 1 small fish
- Vegetable: 1-2 handfuls mixed seaweed
- Fluid: Seaweed provides water content

Eating Protocol
1. Eat slowly
2. Chew thoroughly
3. Stop at first sign of fullness
4. Save 1/3 for later

Post-Meal Actions (While Digesting)
- [ ] Clean tools
- [ ] Note what worked
- [ ] Identify 3 improvements for next meal
- [ ] Plan next 12 hours

Emergency Only: Riskier Options

Only if NO other food found by Hour 5:
- Barnacles: Scrape off rocks, boil if possible
- Sea Anemones: ONLY certain types, cook thoroughly
- Fish Eyes/Roe: Nutrient-dense but small portions
- Sea Cucumbers: Process carefully (remove toxins)

Safety Cut-Offs

Stop Immediately If:
- You feel dizzy or nauseated
- Shellfish smell bad (discard ALL)
- Water conditions worsen
- Light fading (stop before dark)
- Legal authority approaches (be transparent)

Success Metrics for First 6 Hours

✅ Minimum Success: 200+ calories consumed  
✅ Good Success: Protein + seaweed eaten  
✅ Excellent Success: Have preserved food for next meal

Next Steps After First Meal

Hour 6-12 Plan:
1. Build proper drying rack
2. Set overnight tide trap
3. Purify drinking water
4. Secure shelter
5. Plan tomorrow's tides

Common 6-Hour Failures & Fixes

Problem: No shellfish in tide pool  
Solution: Move to rocky outcrop, check lower tide zone

Problem: Can't make fire  
Solution: Use sun-rocks, eat seaweed raw, make ceviche

Problem: Everything tastes bitter  
Solution: You harvested wrong seaweed - find green/red types only

Problem: Got only 2 tiny fish  
Solution: Make fish broth - boil with seaweed for soup
