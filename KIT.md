# KIT.md — Field-Action Readiness and Safety Review Kit

**Critical note:** this repository contains survival-oriented instructions. Do not treat any improvised tool, wild food, water treatment or field technique as safe merely because it is written down. Verify each instruction against authoritative local safety and legal sources before use.

## Read first
- `README.md` and every linked safety/legal warning
- local tide, weather, marine hazard and emergency information
- local laws governing access, collecting, fishing and protected areas
- evidence for each food, water and tool claim

## Requirements and procurement artefacts
For each action record: trigger/decision rule, prerequisites, hazards, stop conditions, tools/materials, legal restrictions, source/date, expected result, failure response and safer alternative. BOM entries need exact specification, quantity, price source/date, availability, and transport cost; mark unknowns honestly.

## Readiness procedure
1. Assess immediate life threats first: injury, exposure, dehydration, weather, tide and access to emergency help.
2. Do not enter surf, unstable rocks, tidal pools or isolated areas to obtain materials.
3. Do not drink untreated natural water or eat unidentified organisms.
4. Do not rely on improvised cutting tools, containers or survival techniques without validated safety evidence.
5. Check local emergency guidance and legal restrictions before any collection or construction.
6. Prefer safe, known supplies and a route to assistance over a speculative self-rescue plan.
7. Record only verified steps and clearly label unresolved claims.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Each executable instruction has an evidence source and context.
- [ ] Hazards, stop conditions and fallback are prominent.
- [ ] Food/water identification and treatment are not assumed.
- [ ] Legal status and local conditions are checked and dated.
- [ ] Procurement claims are sourced.
- [ ] Unsafe or unverified actions are blocked pending review.

**Stop condition:** if the action requires entering dangerous water/terrain, handling unknown organisms, or improvising a blade or water-treatment system, do not proceed on the strength of this document alone.
